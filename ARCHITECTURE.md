# How Ironbooks works

This document explains how each part of Ironbooks works: what it stores, how it posts to the books, which rules always hold and where they are enforced, and where its known limits are. Review round 5 (October 2026) wrote it: one auditor per area read every file of that area in full before reviewing it. Use it to find your way around the code before you change it.

## 1. Ledger core, posting, journal and the database guards

The ledger is one double-entry journal per company: journal_entries (company, entry_number, date stored as a UTC-midnight calendar day, description, reference, status draft/posted/void, source_ref linking the posting to its document such as invoice:<id>, manual, reversal:<id>, fiscal-close:<year>, fixed_asset:<id>, refund:<id>, account:<id>, created_at = transaction start) and journal_entry_lines (account, one positive debit or credit with 2 decimals, memo, class, optional Name entity_type/entity_id, optional foreign amount). Every posting goes through lib/services/posting.ts postJournalEntry: assertBalanced (2dp, finite, within numeric(15,2), one side per line, debits = credits exactly), assertPeriodOpen (Accountant's Copy dividing date, then closed fiscal periods, then the company closing date unless the request carried a valid closing-date password; a password override is recorded on the Closing Date Exception report), company scoping of accounts and classes, non-posting accounts refused, and the A/R / A/P name rule (assertControlAccountNames: a document marks its own control line controlLine; any other A/R line must name a customer and any A/P line a vendor). The entry number is MAX+1 inside the posting transaction (PGlite serializes transactions; indexed since 0063). The database enforces the same invariants itself (drizzle/0017, 0025, 0043, 0063). A deferred constraint trigger checks every touched posted entry at COMMIT (BK001). Lines and headers of posted/void entries are immutable except inside the creating transaction or a txid-bound sanctioned rewrite (condense, merge, restore, reclassify) (BK002). accounts.balance is maintained only by statement triggers, so a direct write must equal the ledger value (BK003). lib/db/ledgerGuards.ts reinstalls every bka_* guard, re-enables triggers, restores dropped or altered foreign keys and removes foreign triggers on every open; checkLedgerHealth then lets lib/db/index.ts refuse a damaged file. lib/db/index.ts opens each company file as its own PGlite folder under an exclusive lock with a heartbeat (lib/db/dirLock.ts). A lost or vanished lock makes the handle refuse every statement and re-check before each COMMIT, and a failed open is now forgotten and its lock released. Postgres data errors become ServiceErrors (pgErrors.ts), and committed audits are appended to the hash-chained transactions.bktlg after COMMIT (txHooks.ts, transactionLog.ts). Services take a ServiceContext; inTransaction/writeAudit block view-only roles and translate BK00x and MoneyParseError errors, and every mutation writes audit_logs, which feeds the transaction log. Edits of posted documents follow postedEdit.ts. If date and line multiset (account, amount, class, name) are unchanged, the entry is kept and only the header and memos change, still behind the closing-date password. Otherwise the entry is voided and re-posted in one transaction, refusing entries with reconciled lines. Manual journal entries (journal.ts) use the same rule in updateEntry, are refused once payments or credits are applied (service check plus the bka_jia_void_guard trigger), and can be reversed or voided only when lib/journalScreen.ts says the entry belongs to the journal window. Named A/R / A/P General Journal lines are customer / vendor open items: journalOpenItems feeds aging and statements, and journalApplications.ts records payments, credits applied to invoices or bills (which change invoice/bill balances but post no GL), and journal-to-journal offsets, all dated so as-of aging ties to the control accounts. cashBasisLedger.ts layers balanced defer/recognize pairs on the accrual ledger per settlement for cash-basis reports. integrity.ts (Verify / Rebuild Data, Books in balance) re-derives cached balances, A/R tie-out and guard status. Period control lives in fiscalPeriods.ts (closed periods, closing date check), company.ts (closing date and password, preferences merged in SQL, secrets redacted), monthEndClose.ts (live checklist, then setClosingDate) and fiscalClose.ts (a year-end closing entry into the Retained Earnings detail-type account, excluded from P&L, voidable but not reversible). Other services: fixedAssets.ts (straight-line schedule; depreciation posts Dr Depreciation Expense / Cr Accumulated Depreciation, resolved by kind and name; voiding the entry removes the period), recurring.ts (memorized transactions entered through each document's own create service under a row lock), undo.ts (30-second undo through the normal services), docNumbers.ts (advisory-locked document numbers), concurrency.ts (record locking via updated_at/edited_at) and whyChanged.ts (number-change explanations from the ledger plus audit trail).

### Rules that always hold

- Every posted entry has 2+ lines, each one positive 2dp side, debits = credits: assertBalanced in posting.ts and bka_assert_entry_balanced at COMMIT (0017)
- accounts.balance equals the posted-line sum: trigger-maintained only (bka_jel_sync_*, bka_je_sync_upd); direct writes refused unless equal to the ledger (bka_accounts_guard); Rebuild Data re-derives
- Posted/void lines and headers are immutable outside the creating transaction or a txid-bound sanctioned rewrite: bka_jel_guard / bka_je_guard / bka_jel_fx_guard
- No line on another company's account or a non-posting account: posting.ts account scoping + BK001 foreign check + bka_jel_non_posting; classes must belong to the company (posting.ts)
- A/R lines name a customer and A/P lines a vendor unless they are a document's own marked control line: assertControlAccountNames (posting.ts) and assertJournalNames (journal.ts)
- Nothing posts or voids on/before the closing date or in a closed period without the closing-date password: assertPeriodOpen on every post/void, assertClosedPeriodEdit for header-only edits; overrides recorded as closing_date_exception audits
- A journal entry with payments/credits applied cannot be voided or edited: assertNoJournalApplications + bka_jia_void_guard (0063, ENABLE ALWAYS)
- Documents are voided/edited through their own document, never from the journal window: isJournalScreenEntry (lib/journalScreen.ts) used by journal.ts and the page; closing entries void only
- All guards are present and ENABLE ALWAYS on every open: ensureLedgerGuards; checkLedgerHealth refuses damaged files (lib/db/index.ts)
- Only one process writes a company folder: dirLock.ts lock + heartbeat; a lost or vanished lock refuses every statement and rolls back at COMMIT; a failed open releases the lock
- Audit trail and transaction log record every mutation after COMMIT only (writeAudit + txHooks afterCommit); the closing-date password hash is never returned or logged (redactCompanySettings / redactSecretsDeep)
- Ledger dates are calendar days at UTC midnight; today on this computer = ledgerToday(); as-of filters include the whole UTC day (onOrBeforeDay / endOfUtcDay)

### Known limits

- Autocommit writes (services writing outside a transaction) are not re-checked against the company-file lock before they run; only transactions re-check at COMMIT, so a non-transactional write in the seconds between another computer's takeover and this process's heartbeat lands in the shared folder
- transactions.bktlg is read with one readFileSync / Buffer-to-string; a log over about 512 MB can no longer be listed or verified
- Rebuild Data 'Remove orphaned audit references' deletes the audit history of journal entries removed by Condense (Condense writes no per-entry delete audit), presenting it as data damage
- Postgres numeric accepts NaN and the triggers / health check count NaN lines as balanced; only service validation (assertBalanced isFinite) keeps NaN out, so a crafted restore could bypass it
- The automatic Entry No. is computed before the posting transaction, so two simultaneous manual entries can get the same Entry No. (QB allows duplicates, low impact)
- Many services outside the ledger resolve default accounts by fixed number (2000, 1050, 1300, 2300, 6900); converted QuickBooks charts number accounts differently
- commitUndo picks the newest audit row by created_at; a JE edit writes void + update rows with the same created_at and relies on index order to pick the update row with replacedBy
- Year-end close can be run for a fiscal year that has not ended; harmless but closes a partial year
- The year-end closing entry is dated 23:59:59Z, which displays as January 1 in time zones east of UTC

## 2. Sales and A/R

Sales/A/R is a set of ServiceContext-scoped services over PGlite/drizzle; API routes are thin zod-validated wrappers (most wrapped in withVersionCheck for record locking). Invoices (invoices.ts) are the core: prepareInvoice prices lines (pricing.resolveLinePricing), classifies item kinds (income/discount/subtotal/payment/sales_tax), computes tax (document rate + per-line rates + tax items), header discount, retainage (held part debited to 1250 Retainage Receivable, an Other Current Asset, never A/R) and payment-item lines (Dr Undeposited Funds inside the invoice entry), then postInvoiceGL posts one penny-allocated balanced entry (sourceRef invoice:<id>, A/R line marked controlLine) in home currency (amounts x exchangeRate); inventory lines post separate COGS entries (invoice-cogs:<id>) through fifo/inventory and site movements. Drafts (pending) post nothing until postDraftInvoice. Edits are void-and-repost inside one transaction (number/createdAt kept, applications kept when still valid); voids reverse GL, COGS/stock, site movements, sales-order claims (read back from the SO audit row), billed time/costs and estimate billing, and now re-check status/payments/deposit inside the transaction. Payments (payments.ts) credit A/R for the full amount (unapplied remainder is a customer credit tracked on paymentsReceived.unapplied), relieve foreign invoices at the booked rate with realized FX to 6900 (payment_fx_reliefs keep dated home reliefs for as-of agings), and post under sourceRef customer:<customerId> (later FX plugs use payment:<id>, refunds refund:<id>, bounces bounced:<id>); applications live in payment_applications, credit-memo applications in credit_applications, General Journal applications in journal_item_applications. Invoice amountPaid/balanceDue/status only change through markPaidAmount (balance against total minus retainage). Sales receipts post deposit/income/tax plus COGS entries that share salesreceipt:<id>; credit memos credit A/R, restock inventory (creditmemo-cogs:<id>, tagged FIFO layers) and are applied/refunded without (apply) or with (refund) GL. Estimates and sales orders never post: progress invoicing links invoice_lines.estimate_line_id and estimateBilling recomputes amount invoiced/status from posted invoices; sales-order conversion claims salesOrderLines.quantityInvoiced with a guarded UPDATE and records per-line claims in the audit trail. Statements (balance-forward rebuilt from documents for home customers, from the GL for foreign-currency customers; open-item from arAgingDetail) must equal customerArLedgerBalance and the A/R aging; finance charges create marker invoices ([FC:YYYY-MM]) posting Dr A/R / Cr 4400 and now charge interest only since the later of the due date and the last finance charge. Retainage billing is a normal invoice with a 1250 line, guarded so Retainage Receivable per customer never goes negative (bill, void and now edit/create). Read-side modules: incomeTracker/collections, letters, fulfillment worksheet (FIFO allocation of on-hand stock to open SO lines), linkedTransactions (Ctrl+H tree), salesForms (PDF/email/print queues via lib/pdf builders), billables (time and reimbursable costs, home amounts), customer/price-level/rule pricing engine. Server-side 'today' for sales documents is the company's calendar day in its time-zone preference (lib/services/salesDates.ts companyToday). Migration 0064 adds the FK/lookup indexes the A/R tables lacked.

### Rules that always hold

- Every A/R posting is balanced and penny-allocated against one home-currency anchor; the document's own A/R line is the only unnamed control line (posting.ts, enforced by DB ledger triggers)
- A/R aging = A/R GL = customer statements = customerArLedgerBalance at any date (customerCredits/fxSubledger as-of reconstruction; statements.ts; tested by tie-out/fuzz suites and sales.review5 section 23)
- Invoice amountPaid/balanceDue/status change only through markPaidAmount, which refuses over-application inside the caller's transaction
- Retainage held stays out of A/R (1250 Other Current Asset); billed retainage per customer never exceeds held (billRetainage, voidInvoice, retainageGuard in create/edit/draft-post)
- Edits are void-and-repost in ONE transaction and must be idempotent when nothing changed (avg-cost reversal uses book value; progress final amounts kept)
- Voids/edits re-read the document inside their transaction before reversing anything (invoices, sales receipts, payments, sales orders)
- Sales-order quantityInvoiced only grows through the guarded claim UPDATE and only shrinks through releaseSalesOrderInvoicingForInvoice; a void order has no invoiced quantities
- Foreign-currency invoices are relieved at the booked rate; credit memos and billable costs are home currency
- Draft invoices post nothing and are excluded from A/R, statements, estimate billing and payments
- Closing date / Accountant's Copy checked by postJournalEntry/voidJournalEntry, assertPeriodOpen on non-posting applications, markPaidAmount (assertAccountantDatesOpen)
- Server-dated sales documents use companyToday() (company time zone), never the UTC day of new Date()

### Known limits

- Billable time/costs are linked to an invoice, not to an invoice line: removing the line in an edit leaves them billed (needs a schema link)
- GET /api/invoices and listInvoices are unbounded; the Invoices page loads every invoice
- Sales-order claims and pre-wave2 credit applications are read back from the audit trail (jsonb); audit rows must never be pruned
- Many as-of computations reconstruct history from current rows plus dated events; any new mutation of applications must record a dated event
- creditApplicationDate fallback, unapply/apply FX plug dates and refund/bounce defaults still use the instant new Date()
- updateEstimate deletes and re-inserts estimate lines; API-created pending invoices linked to them would lose the links
- Estimate sales-tax item lines are never marked billed, so such an estimate cannot close through progress invoicing

## 3. Purchases, A/P, checks, sales tax and 1099s

Purchases / A/P is a set of document services that all post through posting.postJournalEntry (sourceRef identifies the document: bill:<id>, expense:<id>, vendor_credit:<id>, item_receipt:<id>, vendor:<vendorId> for bill payments, tax_agency:<id> for sales tax checks, refund:<creditId> for vendor refunds, item:<itemId> for landed cost) and keep a subledger next to the GL. Bills (bills / bill_lines) post Dr line accounts (inventory lines to the item's asset account and receive stock: FIFO layers tagged bill:<id> via purchaseLayers so layer value == posted amount, or a weighted average with value remainder) / Cr 2000 A/P as one controlLine; foreign bills post one home anchor penny-allocated over the lines. bills.balanceDue = total - amountPaid - amountCredited is the A/P subledger; Pay Bills (billPayments.payBills / payBillsWithCredits) writes bill_payment_applications (amountApplied = cash + discount, amountHome = A/P relieved at the bill's booked rate), posts Dr A/P / Cr bank / Cr discount / FX plug, and numbers checks inside the transaction (checkNumbers.allocateCheckNumber, per bank account, duplicate warning unless confirmed). Vendor credits post Dr A/P / Cr line accounts (inventory returns relieve stock at current cost, price difference to 5000) and are applied to bills without GL (home: audit rows applied_to_bill / unapplied_from_bill are the application history; foreign: vendor_credit_applications rows + realized FX entries in vendorCreditsFx.ts); refunds post Dr bank / Cr A/P (refund:<id>) and voiding that entry restores the credit (posting.restoreRefundedCustomerCredit). Write Checks / card charges (expenses.ts) post Dr lines / Cr bank or card, receive item-line stock like bills, and share the print queue. Purchase orders never post; purchase_order_lines.quantityBilled counts both item-receipt and bill consumption (guarded conditional UPDATEs), quantityReceivedUnbilled drives enhanced receiving, and the PO->bill claim lives on the PO audit row {billId, billedQuantities[{lineId, quantity, billLineOrder}]} plus signed adjustments written when such a bill is edited (poClaimsForBill nets them; voidBill releases the net). Item receipts post Dr inventory / Cr the Item Receipts Accrual (found by name/description, code 2050 unless taken); converting a receipt or Enter Bill for Received Items (receiving.ts) posts Dr accrual / Cr A/P with signed price-variance lines (capitalized into FIFO layers / average cost or PPV/COGS) and item_receipt_bill_lines that voidBill unwinds (receivingLinks.releaseReceiptBilling). Landed cost moves an expense bill line into receipt layers / average cost with one entry per receipt line, reversible only while the stock is untouched. Sales tax: rates/agencies/components (salesTax.ts, combinedTax.ts; combined rate = sum of stored components), Pay Sales Tax writes one payment_checks row + entry per agency (Dr 2200 / Cr bank), and the liability views come from the GL-driven accountant/salesTaxLedger.ts. Pay Liabilities writes liability checks to the accounts the paychecks credited (liabilityAccountResolver). Print Checks (printQueue.ts) lists queued expenses / bill payments / payment checks / paychecks per bank account, stamps numbers through the owning service (closing-date password for closed periods), and checkPrintData refuses voided or non-check documents; one PDF batch is one bank account. 1099s (statements.vendor1099Report / vendor1099Worksheet, form1099Xml) count cash paid in the calendar year (bill payment cash share, checks, General Journal items paid in Pay Bills; card-settled excluded) by the 1099 account mapping, with a $600 threshold through 2025 and $2,000 from 2026 (threshold1099). Edits follow void+repost in one transaction unless ledger-neutral (memo/number/description edits keep the entry: isLedgerNeutralBillEdit, planPostedEdit, assertClosedPeriodEdit); routes wrap edits/voids in withVersionCheck (updated_at record locking) and every route is in lib/permissions/routes.ts (vendors / banking / inventory / sales_tax / payroll / reports_purchases areas).

### Rules that always hold

- Every posting balanced and through postJournalEntry/voidJournalEntry (DB ledger triggers, closing date + Accountant's Copy dividing date in assertPeriodOpen); A/P lines only as the document's controlLine (posting.assertControlAccountNames)
- A/P GL == open bills (balanceDue) - unapplied vendor credits - open GJ A/P items; balanceDue = total - amountPaid - amountCredited (applyRows/rollbackApplications relative SQL; applyToBill/unapply; journal credits creditBill)
- A bill payment and its entry move together: read + void of a payment in one transaction (voidBillPayment), edits repost on the same document (updateBillPayment)
- Inventory GL == stock subledger: purchase layers worth exactly the posted home amount (purchaseLayers), reversals take exactly what the document added (takeFromDocumentLayers), average-cost value remainder (valueRemainderAfter), landed cost / variances post the book-value change
- PO quantityBilled == net claims of receipts + bills from it: guarded conditional UPDATEs (convertToBill, claimReceiptQuantities, syncPoBillingForBillEdit) and releases (releasePoBillingForBill nets audit claims, releaseReceiptQuantities)
- Item Receipts Accrual nets to zero per fully billed receipt (accrual relief = receipt amount on the closing bill line; convert reads lines after the claim)
- Check numbers unique per bank account unless the user confirmed (allocateCheckNumber / assertCheckNumberAvailable inside the saving tx; re-checked when a check moves accounts); checks only from bank accounts (expenses, Pay Bills)
- Print Checks never prints a voided or non-check document, and one PDF batch is one bank account (checkPrintData, /api/checks/pdf)
- Ledger-neutral edits keep the entry, stock and applications and still need the closing-date password (isLedgerNeutralBillEdit, isLedgerNeutralReceiptEdit, planPostedEdit, assertClosedPeriodEdit)
- Company scoping on every referenced id (assertRefInCompany / scoped selects); sensitive vendor fields masked on every return and masked echoes never stored (maskForContext, isMaskedValue)
- 1099 cash per calendar year by the account mapping; form required at threshold1099(year)
- Combined tax rate == sum of its stored (6dp) component rates (setComponents)

### Known limits

- audit_logs has only a (company_id, created_at) index while PO claims, vendor credit applications and undo/version lookups filter by entity/JSON: ~140 ms per lookup at 400k audit rows (measured), growing linearly
- List endpoints for bills, vendor credits, POs, item receipts, landed cost and payment checks return every row (no paging) to pages that load them whole

- Return price differences, PPV-on-sold-share and landed cost on sold units go to 5000, not the item's own COGS account
- The PO->bill claim mapping is positional (billLineOrder); bills converted and reordered before this round refuse a quantity edit (void and re-create)

## 4. Banking, registers, reconciliation and statement import

Banking covers bank links, the bank feed, Make Deposits, Transfer Funds, typed register entries, reconciliation, statement and file import, bank rules and suggestions, the Loan Manager and receipt capture. None of these keep balances of their own: every money movement posts through posting.postJournalEntry (balance, closing date, A/R-A/P name and ledger-trigger guards), and each source document stores its postedEntryId. Postings carry a sourceRef: transfer:<id> (new this round; migration 0066 backfills old transfers), deposit:<id>, bank_transaction:<id> for a feed line that was Added, reconciliation:<id> for statement adjustments, cc-payment:<id>, and loan:<id> for loan FX gain or loss. Because of the sourceRef, the Journal screen will not edit or void those entries apart from their document. A bank_accounts row links exactly one GL account (bank, card or other balance-sheet account; never Undeposited Funds, Inventory Asset, A/R or A/P) to feed staging and to reconciliation. Imports (OFX/QFX/QBO with multi-statement selection by ACCTID, CSV with a column mapper, Excel through readXlsx, PDF or scanned statements through pdfjs, offline tesseract OCR or the company AI) only stage bank_transactions rows. Statement rows must tie out (opening + rows = closing) unless the user acknowledges the difference. Dedupe uses fitId (a sha256 hash fallback when the file has none) and runs in one transaction with the inserts, one savepoint per row. Bank review merges rules (lib/bankRules/core pickRule, which has precedence), the user's history and the local model or an LLM (through lib/ai/router) into one suggestion per line. categorize posts Dr bank / Cr offset on the line's calendar day under a matched=false guard and refuses the feed's own account, Undeposited Funds and Inventory Asset. matchTransaction links a line to an existing posted entry that nets the same amount on the bank GL within 14 days; candidates for every waiting line come from one pass (suggestMatchesForAccount). resyncFeedMatches, run when the feed is opened (runAutoRules), re-points a matched line to its transaction's edited entry or returns it to review when that transaction was voided. Make Deposits relieves Undeposited Funds (code 1050) by consuming payments, sales receipts and invoice payment items (deposit_lines with description prefixes salesReceipt:, invoicePayment: and cashback:). Its list is computed with NOT EXISTS against non-void deposits, and FX deposits relieve UF at each payment's posted home value, with an exchange gain/loss line. Edits of deposits and transfers use postedEdit.planPostedEdit: a ledger-neutral edit keeps the entry (its line memos are synced by entryLineMemos.syncLineMemos), and anything else voids and re-posts in one transaction, refused when a line is in a completed reconciliation. A foreign edit with no rate stated keeps the saved rate. Reconciliation sessions hold reconciliation_items. The cleared balance is bankAccounts.lastReconciledBalance plus the posted cleared lines in statement terms (card: credit minus debit), and a session finishes only at a 0.00 difference (lib/reconcileRules). Undo marks the last session 'undone' and rolls back lastReconciled*. Statement adjustments post and auto-clear in one transaction. The Loan Manager computes schedules in pure Decimal math and writes payments as checks or bills through expenses and bills. Its balance comes from non-void Loan Manager payments only. Receipt capture stores the file in the Doc Center, reads it locally (PDF text, offline OCR, or a local vision model only), then saves it as an expense or bill under a version check and attaches it.

### Rules that always hold

- GL Undeposited Funds (1050) = the Make Deposits list: only payments / sales receipts / invoice payment items put money in and only deposits take it out. Enforced by registerEntryPlan FORM_ONLY_SUBTYPES and subledgerAccountReason in transfers, deposit extra lines / cash back, bank-feed categorize, bank rules, legacy rules, reconcile adjustments, loan escrow and bank links.
- GL Inventory Asset = inventory valuation: the same subledgerAccountReason guard on all those banking paths; posting guard + A/R-A/P name rule for control accounts.
- A payment / receipt is in at most one non-void deposit: checked inside the deposit's transaction (usedPaymentIds etc.); deposit date on or after every item's date; a deposit holds payments in its bank account's currency only.
- Each source document has one live posted entry, linked by sourceRef; edits void + repost in one transaction (or keep the entry when ledger-neutral, syncing line memos); entries with lines in a completed reconciliation can't be voided or re-posted (posting.voidJournalEntry, postedEdit).
- One bank link per GL account and one in-progress reconciliation per link (checked in the inserting transaction).

- Statement rows import only when the tie-out over the exact parsed amounts is 0.00 or the difference was acknowledged (checked in app/api/import/route.ts).
- Money is Decimal (lib/money) on the server; dates are UTC-midnight calendar days; typed dates go through parseDateInput / real-day checks (no rollover of 2026-02-30).

- Bank-feed Add posts a journal-style entry (sourceRef bank_transaction:) without the payee as a vendor name, so vendor reports / 1099 totals don't see bank-feed expenses (QB creates a check / expense for the vendor).
- The review screen reloads the whole feed history (filter=all) plus suggestions after every action; listDeposits / listTransfers / listStaged are unbounded on very large files.
- Undeposited Funds is found by code 1050 in deposits / payments but by subtype in the register / transfer guards; an account whose subtype was changed (accounts service allows it with history) splits the two.
- bank_transactions has no unique index on (bank_account_id, fit_id): only importTransactions' transaction prevents duplicates; the demo feed and other writers don't.

- Loan Manager balance counts only payments set up through it; editing a loan payment's check elsewhere leaves loan_payments stale.
- Statement adjustments stay posted when a reconciliation is cancelled or undone (as in QB); there is no Mark All in the Reconcile window.
- Receipts / statements OCR runs in-request on one shared tesseract worker; a 40-page scan can take a long time.

## 5. Items, inventory and cost layers

Items are master data (lib/services/items.ts): type, linked income / COGS (expenseAccountId) / inventory asset accounts (the asset account must be an Other Current Asset), barcode, serial/lot tracking, U/M set, preferred vendor, sales tax code (items.taxable mirrors the code's flag) and a record-locking version items.edited_at (0067) that ignores stock figures. An inventory item's stock lives in three places that must agree: items.quantityOnHand (company-wide count written by every stock path), the costing subledger, and the GL inventory asset account (item.assetAccountId, else code 1300). Costing is item-level and decided by data: an item with ANY inventory_layers row is FIFO (fifo.ts owns the layers; average-cost paths refuse such items via assertNotFifoTracked), otherwise it is average cost (items.averageCost at 4dp plus items.valueRemainder / valueRemainderAvg, the cents a 4dp average cannot show, relieved with the last unit: inventory.ts valueRemainderAfter / carriedValueRemainder / sellOutRelief). Removals are valued as book value before minus after (bookValueRelief; FIFO per layer with fifoLayerValue) so selling out always clears the GL to exactly $0, and new FIFO layers are made worth exactly what the document posted (insertLayersWorth splits one unit off to carry the cents). Every value movement posts a balanced entry through posting.postJournalEntry (closing date, closed periods, Accountant's Copy dividing date, ledger triggers); movements worth $0 post nothing but still pass zeroValueAdjustment (period check + closing-date exception). Source refs: adjustments, physical counts, value adjustments, FIFO receipts/consumption, opening stock and landed cost use item:<id> (offset = the Adjustment Account, default 'Inventory Adjustments', with Class and Customer:Job; opening stock offsets Opening Balance Equity); invoices post COGS per line as invoice-cogs:<id> + reference cogs:<lineOrder>, sales receipts as salesreceipt:<id> + reference = line id, credit-memo restocks as creditmemo-cogs:<id>, all to the item's own COGS account (cogsAccountForItem); purchases (bills, checks/charges with an Items tab, item receipts, vendor credits) carry the inventory debit/credit on their own entry and only move the subledger through bills.ts receivePurchasedStock / reversePurchasedStock and vendorCredits relieveReturnedStock / restoreCreditStock; builds post assembly_build:/assembly_unbuild:<id>:<from>:<to> value moves between items' asset accounts. Voids/edits put stock back symmetrically: FIFO sales and vendor-credit returns go back into the layers they were drawn from (fifo.ts restoreConsumedStock / restoreLayerDraws, found through the consumeStock audit row, index 0067), purchase reversals take back exactly their own tagged layers (takeFromDocumentLayers; CONFLICT when the units were sold or revalued), with cent true-ups against COGS. Where stock sits is a separate append-only ledger, inventory_movements (inventorySites.ts): a non-default site's quantity is the sum of its movements, the default site's is quantityOnHand minus all other sites, bins/serials/lots are derived from movements and re-validated after every batch (recordMovements / reverseSourceMovements); every stock document writes movements in its own transaction via postDocumentInventory (per-line site/bin/serial/lot details stored in inventory_line_details, carried forward on edits), transfers (inventoryTransfers.ts) move only movements and have no GL. Reports: Inventory Valuation Summary (inventory.ts inventoryValuation, ties to the balance sheet including inactive items and value remainders), valuation as of a date and detail reconstructed from GL lines attributed to items by their source document (loadItemGlMovements), Stock Status / reorder report (inventoryOps.ts; on PO = ordered - billed/received, committed = open SO lines), site reports and the self-check (inventorySiteReports.ts verifySiteInvariants: sites add up, serials unique, GL == valuation), Inventory Center (lists/inventoryCenter.ts). The list services (lib/services/lists/*, typeLists.ts) are company-scoped master data with no GL impact: simple lists (terms, ship via, payment methods, tax codes, ...), customer/vendor types, U/M sets, billing rate levels, notes/to-dos, Add/Edit Multiple (lists/bulk.ts, all-or-nothing savepoints), Clean Up (cleanup.ts) and merges (mergeLists.ts, one transaction, FK repointing driven by the live catalog in references.ts, value-neutral except a rounding entry for average-cost merges); deleting an entry that is referenced anywhere (catalog scan, incl. jsonb) deactivates it instead, and every delete / deactivate / merge is refused while an Accountant's Copy is out.

### Rules that always hold

- GL inventory asset balance == Inventory Valuation Summary (layers per-layer cents + average book value + value remainder): enforced by bookValueRelief / fifoLayerValue / insertLayersWorth / valueRemainderAfter and true-ups; checked by verifySiteInvariants
- An item is FIFO exactly when it has inventory_layers rows; average-cost operations (adjustInventory, recordCOGS, physicalCount, builds) refuse FIFO items (assertNotFifoTracked)
- Sum of site quantities == items.quantityOnHand by construction (default site derived); site / bin / lot quantities never negative and a serial is in stock at most once (inventorySites.assertMovementState after every movement batch, including reversals)
- Every value movement posts through postJournalEntry; a $0 movement still passes the closing date / closed periods / dividing date and records a closing-date password override (zeroValueAdjustment, builds, transfers)
- Void / edit reverses every stock side effect: COGS entries voided and units returned to the layers they came from; purchase reversals take back exactly their own layers or refuse (CONFLICT); site movements reversed with the original date
- A sale always posts COGS > $0 (recordCOGS / consumeStock refuse units that carry no cost) because void/edit find the stock to restore through the COGS entry
- Item type / asset account / U/M set / tracking mode cannot change while the item holds stock or history (items.ts guards)
- Item record lock version is items.edited_at, which stock movements do not move (trigger bka_version_items, concurrency.versionField)
- The default site's reorder point is the item's reorder point (setSiteReorderPoint / setReorderPoint / listSiteReorderPoints / stockStatusBySite)
- Physical count difference = counted - quantity on hand at the count date (today's less later-dated site movements)
- Every query scoped by companyId; foreign ids verified (assertRefInCompany / notFound); list deletes, deactivations and merges refused while an Accountant's Copy is out (assertListDeleteAllowed)
- Merges run in one transaction and never move value between accounts (same asset account required); a referenced list entry is deactivated, never deleted (references.ts catalog scan)

### Known limits

- Perpetual, non-retroactive costing: back-dated sales, receipts and value adjustments are costed at today's average/layers, unlike QB which re-costs forward; a back-dated Adjust Value uses today's quantity and value
- Quantity history for back-dated counts comes from site movements; physical counts and opening stock write none, so a later-dated count entered first, or data from before Advanced Inventory, is not subtracted
- Valuation as of a date and valuation detail derive quantity as value / current unit cost (exact only when the cost has not changed) and cannot attribute manual or imported GL lines on an inventory account to items
- Inventory adjustments and assembly builds have no void / edit (QB allows deleting them); corrections are new adjustments / unbuilds
- Assembly components must be inventory items (QB also allows service / non-inventory / other charge components); FIFO items cannot be counted on the count sheet or built
- U/M sets are list-only: document lines carry no unit, so every quantity is in the base unit (toBaseQuantity is unused)
- FIFO restore after a merge or a layer revaluation falls back to a new layer dated the sale (cost order may change)
- Inventory Center history omits $0 adjustments, counts and opening stock (no journal entry), so its quantities need not add up to on hand for zero-cost stock
- Postgres 23505 unique violations are not translated globally; races outside a transaction elsewhere still surface as HTTP 500

## 6. Payroll

Payroll is after-the-fact payroll built on employees, paychecks and paycheck_lines. A paycheck row stores gross/taxes/deductions/net, entry_type ('paycheck', 'ytd_adjustment' for mid-year history, 'liability_adjustment' for Adjust Liabilities), pay_run_id, voided_at, check number and (migration 0068) periods_per_year; each line has a kind (earning/tax/deduction/employer_contribution), an optional payroll item and a tax tracking code (payrollTaxTracking.ts). The tracking code, never the line name, decides which wage bases a deduction reduces (401k-type: income-tax wages only; Section 125/HSA/dependent care: income-tax and FICA/FUTA/SUI wages) and which W-2 box / 941 line / 940 line an amount lands on. payroll.ts preparePaycheck is the single calculation engine shared by runPaycheck, editPaycheck, previewPaycheck and createPayRun: it builds earnings, resolves items, calls computeWithholding (payrollTax.ts: federal tables 2022-2026, 2020+ and pre-2020 W-4s, SS base by year, Additional Medicare over 200k YTD), state withholding (statePayrollTax.ts: 17 formula states, 9 no-tax states, others employee-entered percent/amount), SDI/SUI/workers comp from payroll_state_settings and workers_comp_codes, and computeEmployerTaxes (employer FICA match, FUTA 0.6% on the first 7,000). Wage bases are applied against YTD wages read INSIDE the posting transaction; pay frequency is the input, else the check's stored frequency, else the company payroll pay period. postPaycheckEntry posts one balanced entry (sourceRef paycheck:<id>): Dr item expense (else Preferences wages account / 6500), Dr employer taxes (6510 else wages), Cr item liability (else Preferences liability account / 2300), Cr Preferences paycheck bank (else 1000) for net; payroll accounts must be home-currency and never A/R/A/P. Edit Paycheck voids the old entry and reposts in one transaction (closing date and reconciliation guards via voidJournalEntry/postJournalEntry); void stamps voided_at, voids the entry and releases any time entries the check paid. Time entries link to paychecks only through a [payroll:<paycheckId>] tag in their description (no FK); paid time is locked (hours/date/employee) and undeletable until the check is voided; billable time is invoiced by timeTracking.billTimeToInvoice through createInvoice (invoicedInvoiceId, cleared by invoice void in invoices.ts). payrollAdjustments.ts holds YTD and liability adjustments (optionally posting, through the same default accounts as paychecks), SUI/SDI settings and workers comp codes. paycheckCountsCondition() defines the population every report uses: live posted checks plus non-posting adjustments, never voided rows. payrollReports.ts derives W-2/W-3 (W-3 = sum of the W-2 state rule, w2StateBoxes), 941 (lines 1-16, Schedule B, line 13 deposits from Pay Liabilities lines whose memo names a 941 item, monthly window or semiweekly replay), 940 (exempt payments, 7,000 base, Schedule A credit reduction by work state), Payroll Summary/Detail, Liability Balances (accruals by item name vs debits to every payroll liability account, ties to GL), sick/vacation accruals (derived from earning-line hours, as of a date), earnings summary, item detail, by payee, state wage worksheet and workers comp summary. Pay Liabilities (liabilityPayments.ts, shared with sales tax) writes Liability Checks that debit the account each item actually credited. Mileage is a non-posting log whose default rate is the IRS business rate for the trip's year (mileageRates.ts); expense reports go draft -> submitted -> reimbursed and post Dr line accounts / Cr 1000 on today's local calendar day. The employee portal (app/portal, /api/portal/*, lib/auth portal tokens bound to the portal password version and the company file id) shows an employee only their own live paychecks and pay stub PDFs (lib/pdf/paystub.ts), and the employee API never returns the portal password hash. PDFs (pay stub, W-2, 941, 940, W-3) use standard fonts through winAnsiText and are stamped SANDBOX in a sandbox copy. Feature switch payrollEnabled gates /api/payroll, /api/pay-liabilities and /api/payroll-items; time and mileage sit under the time permission area and timeTrackingEnabled.

### Rules that always hold

- Every paycheck entry is balanced: gross = net + taxes + deductions, and employer taxes are posted once as a debit and once as a credit (postPaycheckEntry; posting.assertBalanced plus the ledger triggers).
- Report population = paycheckCountsCondition(): live posted paychecks plus non-posting YTD/liability adjustments, never voided rows. W-2/W-3/941/940/stubs/YTD all use it (payroll.ts).
- Wage bases and form boxes come from tax tracking codes (paycheckWageBases/lineTracking), never free-text names. Legacy rows are inferred once from their names.
- The Social Security / FUTA / SUI / SDI wage bases and the Additional Medicare threshold are applied against YTD wages read in the same transaction that posts or edits the check (runPaycheck/editPaycheck call preparePaycheck(tx)).
- A paycheck is withheld at its own pay frequency. Edits reuse paychecks.periods_per_year, and when no frequency is given the company payroll pay period applies (resolvePeriodsPerYear).
- Payroll default accounts (Preferences wages/liability/paycheck bank) are of the right kind and in the home currency, and payroll items never map to A/R, A/P or non-posting accounts (preferredAccountId, payrollItems.assertAccountInCompany).
- Time paid on a check carries [payroll:<paycheckId>]. It cannot be paid twice (pay run tx re-check plus markTimeEntriesPaidInPayroll), and its hours/date/employee cannot change and it cannot be deleted while paid (timeTracking). Voiding the check releases it (releaseTimeEntriesFromPaycheck).
- The Payroll Liability Balances report equals the GL balance of the payroll liability accounts (payrollLiabilityAccountIds). This is tested in tieout.review4 and payrollWave2.retest.
- W-3 Box 16 equals the sum of the W-2 Box 16 amounts (shared w2StateBoxes). A state without income tax on wages and nothing withheld leaves Boxes 15-17 empty.
- Edit and void go through voidJournalEntry/postJournalEntry, so the closing date (with password), closed periods, completed reconciliations and the Accountant's Copy dividing date are enforced. The employee and paycheck routes use withVersionCheck record locks.
- Document dates are the local calendar day stored at UTC midnight: invoices from time, expense reimbursements, mileage defaults and report defaults.
- Portal: tokens are bound to the portal password version and the company file id, an employee sees only their own live paychecks and stubs, and the portal password hash is never sent to the browser (employees routes maskEmployee).
- Money uses Decimal (lib/money). Typed numbers are validated as finite before storage (rates, wage bases, expense amounts, hours).

### Known limits

- Liability payments are attributed to items by line memo = item name (liabilityPayments, payrollLiabilityBalances, 941/940 deposits). A renamed item or a lump-sum payment cannot be attributed, and lump sums never count on 941/940 line 13.
- The time-to-paycheck link is a description tag, not a foreign key. Anything that rewrites time_entries.description outside timeTracking.ts can break it.
- State coverage: only 17 states are modelled for 2025/2026. Several tables are flagged unconfirmed (UNCONFIRMED_TABLES, e.g. GA 2026), and local taxes (PA EIT, NYC, Ohio cities, Indiana counties) are not calculated.
- Federal 2026 tables include no W-4 or 2025 reconciliation-act withholding changes beyond the brackets and standard deductions (no-tax-on-tips/overtime deductions are not modelled).
- Paychecks created before migration 0068 have NULL periods_per_year. Editing one recalculates at the company payroll pay period, which may differ from the frequency it was originally run at.
- The semiweekly 941 deposit allocation replays the company's whole payroll history on every 941. liabilityPayments.liabilityAccountForItem loads every non-earning paycheck line once per item paid. Both are performance cliffs on large files.
- Sick/vacation accruals only grow: used hours are not tracked (no sick/vacation pay items).
- A reimbursed expense report cannot be voided or deleted (the journal screen refuses voiding document entries and there is no expense-report void).
- employees.w4 jsonb is only partly validated (filing status, state W-4). Bad Step 3/4 amounts silently count as 0.
- Payroll PDFs are simplified informational copies: Box 12 boxes only for the first 4 codes, long 941/940 notes are not wrapped, and non-Latin-1 text is transliterated or replaced with '?'.
- Inactive payroll items can still be referenced by id through the API on a new paycheck.

## 7. Multiple currencies

Ironbooks keeps a single ledger in home currency. Every foreign-currency document (invoice, payment, bill, bill payment, check/card charge, vendor credit, deposit, transfer, loan) stores its amounts in its own currency plus an exchange rate (home units per 1 foreign unit, 8 decimals; payments_received widened from 6 to 8 in 0070) and posts its GL lines in home currency: one anchor round2(total x rate) penny-allocated across the lines (currencies.homeAmounts / money.allocate), so every entry balances. A typed rate wins, otherwise the currency list's single current rate is used (exchangeRates.currencyRate, currencies.resolveDocumentCurrency, payments.resolvePaymentCurrency, invoices.resolveInvoiceCurrency); edits with a blank rate keep the saved rate. Bank and credit-card accounts may have their own currency (accounts.currency); postJournalEntry calls fxLines.foreignLineColumns, which writes journal_entry_lines.currency / foreign_amount / exchange_rate for every line on such an account (documents pass the exact typed amount, anything else is converted at the entry's or the list's rate; Home Currency Adjustment lines carry foreign_amount 0 = home value only). Registers, bank feeds, matching and reconciliation read the account currency through fxLedger (accountDebitSql, accountSigned, withAccountCurrency). A foreign A/R or A/P item stays at its booked rate until settled: each settlement records the home it relieved (payment_fx_reliefs + payment_applications.amount_home for invoices, bill_payment_applications.amount_home for bills, vendor_credit_applications.home_bill / home_credit for credits); the application that pays an item off relieves exactly the remaining home amount and the difference is realized gain/loss on the Exchange Gain/Loss account (found by name, currencies.getOrCreateExchangeGainLossAccount). fxSubledger rebuilds, as of any date, each open foreign invoice, unapplied foreign payment, bill and vendor credit with its foreign open amount and the home amount the control account carries, plus Home Currency Adjustment items between an adjustment and its next-day reversal; A/R and A/P aging, aging detail, open invoices, the integrity check and Home Currency Adjustment all read it, so the subledger ties to the GL on every date. customerCredits rebuilds unapplied customer payments as of a date (refunds and foreign applies dated later are added back) for the A/R credit side. Home Currency Adjustment (homeCurrencyAdjustment.ts) revalues foreign bank/card balances permanently (in date order; void latest first), and foreign A/R per customer, A/P per vendor and foreign loans with a next-day reversal; it reads balances inside its posting transaction and updates the currency list only when it is not older than the list rate. Foreign payment refunds, voided refunds and back-dated refunds keep A/R equal to the credit at every date through dated FX plugs (posting.postPaymentCreditFxPlugs, sourceRef payment:<id>). Deposits relieve Undeposited Funds at the home amount each payment posted and book the deposit-day gain/loss; transfers carry one home amount and each side's own-currency amount; loans book principal at the loan rate and post realized gain/loss per payment (sourceRef loan:<id>), the payoff clearing the remainder. Foreign customers get home-currency statements tied to home A/R (including FX plugs and HCA lines). Multiple Currencies (A2, multiCurrency.ts) is off for new files, refuses foreign saves while off, and locks on (and the home currency is fixed, currencies.setBaseCurrency) once foreign data exists. Credit memos, sales receipts, journal items (GJ charges/credits), item receipts and Loan payments from mismatched accounts are home-currency only and refused against foreign documents or accounts.

### Rules that always hold

- Every posting is in home currency and balanced: anchor round2(total x rate) penny-allocated over the lines (homeAmounts / allocate); the ledger triggers reject anything else.
- Rates are home units per foreign unit, > 0, <= 1,000,000, 8 decimals everywhere (parseRate, listRateOf, resolvePaymentCurrency); a payment at its invoice's rate books no gain/loss.
- Lines on foreign bank/card accounts carry the account-currency amount (foreignLineColumns); documents pass the exact amount; HCA lines carry 0. A line on a bank/card account in a THIRD currency is refused on checks, bills, vendor credits (assertLineAccountsInDocumentCurrency), bank-feed categorize, register entries and Receive Payments deposit-to.
- A/R / A/P control (home) = fxSubledger + home items as of any date; foreign items are relieved at the booked rate and the payoff relieves exactly what is left (payments.ts, billPayments.ts, vendorCreditsFx.ts, loans.ts); realized differences go to the Exchange Gain/Loss account found by name.
- The home currency cannot change once foreign data exists (setBaseCurrency) and Multiple Currencies cannot be turned off then (assertMultiCurrencyChangeAllowed); while off, every foreign save is refused (assertForeignCurrencyAllowed at the currency choke points).
- A foreign vendor / bank account only takes documents in its currency (resolveDocumentCurrency); account currency changes only while unused (setAccountCurrency), vendor currency only while it has no transactions (vendorPurchaseFields).
- Edits with a blank rate keep the saved rate (invoices, deposits, transfers, vendor credits, loans, deposited payments; the bill, check, payment and Pay Bills forms re-send it).
- Home Currency Adjustment: computed inside its posting transaction; bank/card revaluations in date order (no back-dated one under a later one; void the latest first); A/R / A/P / loan part reversed the next day; the pair is not a General Journal open item once reversed; the currency list is only overwritten by an adjustment not older than the list rate.
- Statements of foreign customers are home-currency and tie to customerArLedgerBalance on every date (FX plugs and HCA lines listed).
- Large files: IN (...) lists in fxSubledger and customerCredits are chunked at 2000 ids.

### Known limits

- One rate per currency (no dated rate history as in QB): defaults always use today's list rate; back-dated work must type the rate.
- The as-of reconstructions (payment_fx_reliefs, dated vendor credit rows, refund audits) are intricate; legacy rows from before 0043 can leave 1-cent residues (documented in ensureLegacyFxRelief).
- Manual journal entries on a foreign account without a foreign amount are converted at the list rate.
- A foreign payment refunded from a home bank is booked at the payment rate (no refund-day rate, no realized gain/loss).
- HCA carries a partly paid foreign loan at round2(balance x loan rate), which can differ by cents from what its liability carries (reversed next day).
- Undeposited Funds holding foreign payments and Retainage Receivable of foreign invoices are not revalued by HCA; retainage of a foreign invoice is billed in home currency.
- Several reports outside the subledger sum document line amounts without the rate (purchases by item, job profitability, unbilled costs by job) - see handoffs.
- A/R, A/P, Undeposited Funds, Inventory and COGS are resolved by account code (1200/2000/1050/1300/5000) across services; a converted file must keep those numbers.

## 8. Reports

The report area is read-only: it never posts. Every amount comes from POSTED journal_entry_lines joined to journal_entries (status 'posted', company-scoped), or from subledger tables for the open-item reports. Money is Decimal throughout (lib/money). reportEngine.ts is the shared engine. It maps account type/subtype to QB statement sections, does the sub-account roll-up (buildAccountTree) and the P&L/BS row layouts, and splits periods by the fiscal year (fiscalClose.fiscalYearWindow + companies.settings.fiscalYearEnd). Its detail-line reader fetchGlLines resolves the QB Type/Num/Name/Split/Clr columns from journal sourceRef prefixes (invoice:, bill:, expense:, paycheck:, bank_transaction: ...), line-level entity names, the transfers table and reconciliation items. It also applies every report filter, caps reads at 10k/50k lines and flags truncation, and lays out the cash-basis view on top of the ledger. The ~90 standard reports are listed in lib/reports/catalog.ts. reportRegistry.ts maps each to a runner, checks the report's permission area and applies catalog defaults. It resolves relative date macros on this computer's local calendar day, defaults the basis from the company preference, validates params with zod (lib/validation/reports.ts), adds truncation notes and remembers the run for tooltips. One route, /api/reports/std/[report], serves them all to app/reports/_components/StdReportView (filters live in the URL; QuickZoom, explanations, memorize, Fonts & Numbers). reportsStandard.ts holds every P&L variant, the Balance Sheet and the Trial Balance. The Balance Sheet is balanced by construction: Retained Earnings and Net Income are split at the fiscal year start, and each column is re-asserted against the ledger. The Trial Balance follows QuickBooks: income and expense accounts show fiscal year to date, earlier years roll into Retained Earnings, on both bases (trialBalanceAmounts, also used by the Combined Trial Balance). The same file has the General Ledger, P&L/BS Detail, Journal and transaction lists. reports.ts keeps the asserted ledger primitives (trialBalance, profitAndLoss, balanceSheet); income statements exclude fiscal-close entries. The cash basis comes from cashBasisLedger.ts (owned by another area). It produces balanced adjustments that activityByAccount, fetchGlLines and balanceSheetCashBasis.ts lay over the accrual ledger. A/R and A/P open items are rebuilt as of any date in reportsExtra.ts (arAgingDetail / apAgingDetail): payment applications by payment date, credit memo / vendor credit activity after the cutoff (home vendor-credit applications come from the audit log), bounced-check snapshots, General Journal open items and the FX subledger. reportsAging.ts (aging, Open Invoices, Collections) and Unpaid Bills reuse them, so these reports tie to the A/R and A/P control accounts. reportsLists.ts covers customer/vendor balances (summed per name in SQL), sales and purchases from document lines (home currency, COGS as posted), list reports and Bills & Applied Payments. incomeTaxReports.ts maps accounts to tax lines; reportsJobsTime.ts does job profitability, time and mileage from document lines. budgets.ts handles budget CRUD plus Budget Overview and Budget vs. Actual (fiscal months, by class or customer:job, balance-sheet budgets). consolidation.ts combines the companies in the file that the user may open. reportDrilldown.ts rebuilds the transactions behind any amount using each report family's own rules, so explanations tie to the cell. reportSummary.ts writes the 'what changed' line; AI phrasing goes through lib/ai/router and every number in the reply is verified. memorizedReports.ts stores report configs, groups and schedules. runDueSchedules writes CSV/XLSX exports next to the company file (single-file companies) or next to the data folder. Exports: lib/export (xlsx, PDF), lib/csv (formula-safe, BOM) and POST /api/export/reports render exactly the table on screen. dashboard.ts and attention.ts read the same services for the home page and the Needs-attention inbox. Ties to other areas: posting/journal (sourceRef conventions), cashBasisLedger, fxSubledger, vendorCreditsFx, customerCredits, payroll (paychecks), banking (bank_transactions, reconciliations), deposits and printQueue.

- Only status='posted' journal entries count; void/draft documents are excluded from document-based reports.

- Balance sheet and trial balance balance by construction and are re-asserted (ledgerAssert assertBalanceSheetBalanced / assertTrialBalanceBalanced; buildBSRows compares laid-out totals with ledger totals per column).
- Dates are calendar days stored as UTC midnight; 'to'/'asOf' bounds are end of that day; 'today' is the local calendar day (reportEngine localToday/endOfToday, reportsExtra endOfLocalToday); audit timestamps are filtered and shown on the local clock (lib/reports/dates.ts).
- Totals are home currency: foreign documents count at their booked rate or the home amounts they posted (homeAmountsByLine, homeOf, homeValue, FX subledger in aging).
- A capped read sets truncated and the page notes that amounts are incomplete (fetchGlLines truncated, executePreparedReport TRUNCATED_NOTE, pivot/find/BvA/transaction lists).
- Drill-down families rebuild cells with the same rules as the runners (row membership, windows, basis, filters, TB roll-up); reportDrilldown tests assert ties.
- Permissions: runStandardReport/prepareStandardReport require the catalog area 'view'; every route is in lib/permissions/routes.ts; schedules need file_utilities plus the report areas; combined reports include only companies the user may open.
- Exports never execute content: CSV cells formula-guarded (lib/csv), XLSX inline strings only with XML-invalid chars stripped, PDF text limited to the font's charset, download names sanitized; schedule folders/names pass safeSegment and never collide.

### Known limits

- Legacy API-only implementations (reportsExtra legacy functions, reportsComparative, classReports, balanceSheetClassified, legacy routes) duplicate std-engine logic and can drift from it; the UI uses the std engine.
- reportDrilldown duplicates each report family's rules; any change to a runner must be mirrored there (tie tests guard the main families only).
- Detail and name reports cap reads at 10k/50k lines; very large files get 'incomplete' notes rather than full results; P&L by Job refuses more than 100 job columns.
- The cash-basis ledger is rebuilt on every cash-basis report run (and per drill-down) - the main cost on large files.
- Report builder (reportBuilder.ts) still reads every matching ledger line into memory instead of aggregating in SQL.
- Aging reconstruction depends on many subledgers (payment/credit applications by date, audit-log vendor credit applications, bounced snapshots, FX subledger); a new settlement path elsewhere must be added there or aging stops tying to A/R and A/P.
- Attention/reminders use the company time-zone preference (default America/New_York) while reports use this computer's local day; they can disagree for users in other zones in the evening.
- Several posting sources (reconciliation charges/interest, assembly builds, opening quantities, HCA, sales tax adjustments) are labelled Type 'Other' on detail reports.

## 9. Accountant tools, Accountant's Copy, preferences and setup

The accountant area is a set of tools that sit on top of the posting engine. Accountant side (accountant.ts): the file is restored and a baseline of versions/digests is recorded; the changes are derived from those (journal edits are chains of voids and re-posts) and exported as a .ibcx. importChangesFile on the client re-applies every change through the normal services in one transaction, under runAccountantImport (only that copy's lock is lifted) with record-lock checks. The route gate (routeGate.ts) refuses, in the accountant's copy, any save the changes file cannot carry back; reads and housekeeping (preferences/my, to-dos, company-file save, price quotes, registers) are allowed. Preferences: lib/preferences.ts holds the defaults, the resolver and FEATURE_GATES (enforced per API path by lib/featureGate.ts from getServerContext). lib/prefBehaviors.ts decides the client-side save prompts (date warnings, warn when editing), the beep and report refresh. The setup interview applies a plan from lib/onboarding.ts through updateCompany/createAccount in one transaction. The AI parts are local first and go through lib/ai/router.ts: the learned categorizer trains on the company's own history; the assistant uses read-only report tools, each gated by its report's view right, and its writes are only pending proposals; the LLM error corrector proposes a correcting journal entry that the user approves. To-dos are one store that feeds the To Do list, Calendar, Reminders and the attention inbox; names of linked records are resolved one query per record type. The command bar parses plain-English dates and intents (lib/commands) against the company calendar day.

### Rules that always hold

- Every ledger change goes through postJournalEntry/createManualEntry or a document service. Reclassify is the only in-place rewrite and runs under allowLedgerRewrite; amounts never change (deferred balance triggers at COMMIT).
- Control accounts (bank, A/R, A/P, credit card, inventory, Undeposited Funds 1050, Sales Tax 2200, payroll 2300, agency liability accounts) can be neither the source nor the target of reclassify, and cannot be the other side of a sales tax adjustment (controlAccountIds).
- A/R and A/P lines carry a customer/vendor: Batch Enter journal rows require the Name; AI proposals resolve names; createManualEntry enforces the name rule.
- Closing date and dividing date are enforced in assertPeriodOpen (fiscalPeriods + accountantCopy/guard). The import of an accountant's changes lifts only that copy's lock (runAccountantImport, an AsyncLocalStorage scope).
- In an accountant's working copy, only saves on ACCOUNTANT_COPY_ALLOWED_API/EXACT may run (routeGate, called from getServerContext). Services also refuse sources the changes file can't carry (accountantMayPost).
- The route map only guarantees one area. Services check the other rights themselves: Batch Enter/Void per document area, Client Data Review apply needs customers/vendors modify, assistant tools need their report's view right, payment reminder needs customers print.
- A save and its audit row commit together (inTransaction). writeAudit refuses viewer contexts, so a refused save changes nothing. Exceptions that change no books audit under a role-stripped context: My Preferences, payment reminders.
- Report day filters on timestamps use the company time zone (companyDayBound); document dates are UTC-midnight calendar days.
- The sales tax engine ties to the GL: sum of agency balances equals the 2200 (+ agency accounts) GL balance on accrual basis; amounts are penny-allocated with Decimal.
- Id lists sent to IN (...) are chunked (1,000) wherever the list is unbounded (sales tax forms, closing-date exception amounts); categorizer training joins in SQL. PGlite refuses more than 65,535 bound parameters.

### Known limits

- Accountant's Copy import (client.ts ~2,000 lines, docs.ts, extras.ts) is large and tested mostly through end-to-end suites; new document fields added elsewhere must be added to DOC_SHAPES/digest or they are silently not carried.
- PGlite has no autovacuum: query plans depend on lib/db/plannerStats.ts running ANALYZE on open and after bulk operations. A file that grows a lot inside one session can hit quadratic plans (seen: the sales tax line scan took 8 s for 20k rows without stats, 60 ms with them).
- Several services still pass unbounded id lists to inArray on paths assumed small (attention overdue-invoice audit lookup, Accountant's Copy name maps); a very large file could hit the 65,535-parameter limit there.
- Recurring routes (/api/recurring, /api/ai/recurring) need only Lists rights, yet can create auto-entering bill/check templates and show vendor amounts.
- Custom field values on invoices are not checked against the closing date or the Accountant's Copy dividing date.
- Reminders totals add foreign-currency balances to home-currency ones.

## 10. QuickBooks migration, imports and exports

The imports area moves a QuickBooks Desktop company into Ironbooks and back out. (1) Lists: qbImport.importIIF parses an IIF file (parseIIF: '!' header rows name the columns of the data rows below) and imports in dependency order: account hierarchy matched by full 'Parent:Sub' path, classes, early registry lists (TERMS, CTYPE/VTYPE, SALESREP, OTHERNAME... via qbIifLists), customers (Customer:Job -> sub-customer + job), vendors, items (QNTY/VALUE opening stock through lists/openingStock), employees, late lists (BUD budgets per fiscal year, TODO), and finally TRNS/SPL transactions posted through postJournalEntry with sourceRef 'iif:<type>:<num>:<date>:<total>:<name>:<occurrence>'. The CSV/Excel list importers (importCustomersCSV/VendorsCSV/ItemsCSV/AccountsCSV, importListXlsx) share the same row importers. These are deliberately NOT one transaction: every row is checked (duplicate, validation, data exception via qbImportErrors.importRowError) and becomes an issue in ImportCounts; idempotency comes from sourceRefs and name/path checks. (2) History: qbGlImport turns the QB General Ledger report (xlsx/csv/pdf -> grid in qbGlFiles) into accounts and balanced transactions (qbGlParse.parseGlGrid + analyzeGl: type inference, running-balance sign votes, self-checks, rebuild by Split pairs/stars/subsets) and runGlImport, in ONE transaction, creates/maps accounts, classes and names, posts the beginning balances as one 'qbgl:open:' entry the day before the period and every transaction as 'qbgl:<fingerprint>:<occ>', then proves that each mapped account's qbgl balance at period end equals the report (and the optional Trial Balance); any difference rolls back unless the user accepts posting it to QB-SUSPENSE; dry-run always rolls back; the proof is stored in qb_gl_imports. Every A/R line carries a customer and A/P line a vendor (qbNameResolver, customerKeyIndex for 'Customer:Job' paths); 'qbgl:' and 'iif:' entries are 'import' sources, so their named A/R/A/P lines are not General Journal open items in aging. (3) Open items: openItemsImport reads QB Open Invoices / Unpaid Bills reports (or typed balances, or IIF INVOICE/BILL blocks with PAID/open balance) and creates real invoices / credit memos / bills / vendor credits with one line against a conversion account: Opening Balance Equity on fresh books, or 'QB-CONV' clearing plus a 'qbopen-offset:' mirror entry when a GL import already holds the balance, so A/R and A/P totals do not change; duplicates are recognised by [qbopen:hash] / [qbdoc:hash] memo tags and audit-log markers; arApReconciliation proves control = aging. IIF payments/journals touching A/R/A/P are applied to the open documents (collectIifControlRows) and their control rows are re-pointed to the conversion account. (4) Bank files: import.importTransactions stages OFX/QFX/QBO/CSV and reviewed statement rows (statementImport extracts PDF text/OCR/xlsx, lib/statements/parse ties out opening + rows = closing) into bank_transactions (no GL posting), deduped by fitId or a date|description|amount|occurrence hash, inside one transaction. Exports: listExport (CSV/xlsx/IIF, formula-safe CSV, Balance from aging) and iifExport (lists in QB codes, general journal TRNS/SPL, sensitive ids only for allowed users). Merge: merge.ts mergeCustomers/Vendors/Accounts re-point every FK (including posted journal lines under allowLedgerRewrite('merge')) in one transaction and deactivate the duplicate; refuses mixed currencies, different QB account types, non-posting accounts and merging away an account the app posts to by code. All QB imports run one at a time per company (importLock.ts) and need owner/admin + single-user (assertExclusiveAccess in the routes). Text files are decoded with lib/textDecode (UTF-16/UTF-8 else Windows-1252) on server, browser and Electron.

### Rules that always hold

- Every rebuilt GL transaction balances (analyzeGl throws INTERNAL otherwise) and posts through postJournalEntry; ledger triggers keep balances.
- GL import is all-or-nothing: one transaction; any account whose qbgl balance at period end differs from the report rolls back unless acceptVariance (posts to QB-SUSPENSE); a Trial Balance difference always rolls back (qbGlImport.runGlImportUnanalyzed).

- A/R lines carry a customer and A/P lines a vendor on GL and IIF imports (controlNames in qbGlImport, ensureIifName in qbImport, resolver side rules).
- An IIF transaction's A/R/A/P rows are applied to open documents exactly when the transaction itself is posted (collectIifControlRows and importIifTransactions share bucketAccountOrProblem, dup and period checks).
- Open items never double count: alreadyInGl documents are netted by qbopen-offset mirror entries, and the import refuses items larger than the control balance without documents (runConversion guard); control = aging proven by arApReconciliation.
- Item opening stock is not posted when the inventory asset account's balance already comes from the same file or a GL import (carriedAccountIds / glImportedAssetAccountIds).
- Amounts are decimal (lib/money) and must look like money: readBankAmount, parseAmount, parseIifAmount accept a comma only as the US thousands separator and never NaN/Infinity; decimal-comma reports are refused.
- Bad input is a 400: data exceptions -> row issues or VALIDATION (qbImportErrors), damaged/oversized xlsx -> VALIDATION (qbGlFiles.readXlsx), statement rows that are not objects -> unreadable (tieOut).
- QB imports into one company run one at a time (importLock.withImportLock in importIIF, list imports, importOpenItems, runGlImport); bank staging dedupe+insert is one transaction.
- Imports need owner/admin and single-user mode (assertExclusiveAccess in /api/import/qb, /api/qb-import, /api/qb-import/gl import+dry-run, /api/qb-import/open-items import); every route is in lib/permissions/routes.ts under file_utilities (merge under lists:delete).
- Merge never moves an account the posting services find by code (CODE_POSTED_ACCOUNTS), never mixes currencies or QB account types, and re-points posted lines only under allowLedgerRewrite('merge').

### Known limits

- importLock is in-process: correct for the embedded single-host PGlite server, not for several server processes on one database.
- IIF and list imports are not transactional by design: a crash mid-file leaves a partial import (re-running completes it idempotently).
- Open-items report imports key a document by its open amount: re-importing a NEWER report where an invoice's open balance changed creates a second document (only IIF documents have docKey change handling).
- Lists IIF export writes current QNTY/VALUE; importing it and the separately exported journal IIF into a new file counts inventory twice (only same-file or GL-import carry is detected).
- GL CSV with ';' separators (Excel in comma-decimal locales) is not sniffed by readCsv and is refused as 'no GL headings'; xlsx is the reliable path.
- GL proof is as of the period end only; per-date correctness relies on every rebuilt entry keeping its report date.
- tagExists / existingDocOwed use LIKE '%[tag]%' scans per item: slow for very large conversions.
- Accounts the app posts to by code can still be renumbered or deactivated outside merge (accounts service) with the same effect merge now refuses.
- Merge re-points history in closed periods without asking for the closing-date password (QB behaviour not verified).

## 11. The desktop app and the company file

The desktop shell (electron/main.js) is a launcher plus file manager around a Next.js standalone server that it spawns on 127.0.0.1; the server owns the PGlite database. A company is either a single file 'ABC.ironbooks' (current format) or an older company folder. A single file is a plain zip (electron/companyFileFormat.js): first a stored manifest (ironbooks-company.json: fileId, saveId/previousSaveId, savedBy, fingerprint, and now dbSchema), then a .bka backup manifest (so a company file is also a valid full backup), then the PGlite data folder, attachments/ and the running transaction log. Opening a file takes the 'in use' lock (electron/companyLock.js: atomic mkdir of '<file>.lock/' next to the file, owner.json heartbeat every 10 s, stale after 2 min measured by the file server's clock, requests/ and responses/ for access requests). It then prepares a private working copy under %LOCALAPPDATA%/Ironbooks/Working/<slug>-<hash of path> (electron/companySession.js planOpen: extract / reuse / recover / conflict, decided from state.json and the manifest saveId) and starts the server with BKA_COMPANY_FILE/STATE/LOCK/LOCK_TOKEN. The server (lib/services/companyFileSave.ts) autosaves the working copy back into the file: 3 s after the transaction-log fingerprint moves, at the latest 30 s after the first change, every 5 min for housekeeping-only changes (measured by the WAL position), and finally on close. Each save writes '<file>.saving-xxxx', fsyncs it and renames it over the file, so the file on disk is always a complete save. A save is refused when owner.json carries another token (the lock was lost). When the file's saveId is no longer the working copy's base (someone else saved), the changes go into one 'unsaved changes' copy next to the file instead. Crash recovery uses state.unsavedSince, plus the transaction-log fingerprint of a working copy that never closed. Per-PC state is config.json (open file, recent files, last user per file, hosting, sandbox flag, theme; written atomically and only after the open file has closed), userData/backups (default backup folder), userData/tls (self-signed LAN certificate), userData/sandboxes, the working copies, and each data folder's .auth-secret. The .auth-secret is excluded from the company file, so sessions are per PC; lib/ai/keyVault.ts also derives AI-key encryption from it, which is a known portability bug (handed off). Things that travel inside the file: .file-id (session binding, and now the identity condense archives are found by), .backup-history.json and .backup-settings.json. Copies (Save Copy As, unsaved-changes copies) no longer carry the history, and rotation only touches backup files visible on this PC. Backups (lib/services/backup.ts): full .bka = adm-zip of the data folder minus FILE_LOCAL_FILES; per-company .bka = JSON rows, re-inserted with fresh ids in one transaction. Both now record dbSchemaAt and are refused when newer. restoreBackup validates, writes a pre-op safety copy, extracts to a sibling temp folder, closes the DB, swaps folders, then verifies the ledger guards and books and rolls back on failure; autosave of a single file pauses meanwhile (restoreState.ts). Versioned backups (backupSchedule.ts) run in the server on the file's schedule plus at quit (main.js runAutoBackup) and rotate keep-N; they skip when the fingerprint is unchanged and fall back to this PC's default folder when the chosen folder is unreachable. Condense (condense.ts) requires a closed period and first writes a verified archive .bka: beside the data folder for company folders, beside the company file for single files, indexed by .file-id. Period Copy, Portable file (.bkp) and Sandbox: periodCopy.ts builds a trimmed .bkp through a portable restore into a temp dir plus condense; portableFile.ts produces a logical per-table export verified by hashes and trial balance; sandbox.ts makes a copy of the whole data folder, the server switches BKA_DATA_DIR to it and the shell relaunches with config.sandbox, and while in it backups, hosting and outbound effects are blocked. Database layer: lib/db openDb now refuses a database whose newest migration is newer than the app's journal (NewerCompanyFileError) before migrate or ensureLedgerGuards can touch it. Multi-user: hosting.js runs a reverse proxy in front of the 127.0.0.1 server. In tailnet mode it listens on the Tailscale IP; in office LAN mode it listens on 0.0.0.0, accepts only private peers and serves HTTPS with a self-signed cert whose fingerprint is published in owner.json and pinned by the joining PC. The proxy strips x-bka-internal and stamps x-bka-remote-addr, so the server applies the remote rules. Access requests: the requester writes requests/<id>.json; the holder answers Let Them In (switches to multi-user and responds with the host URLs and fingerprint), Hand It Over (saves, closes and releases the lock) or Not Now. requestAccess never re-creates a released lock.

### Rules that always hold

- The company file on disk is always one complete save: built as a temp file in the same folder, fsynced, renamed (companyFileFormat.writeCompanyFile); temp files of cut-short saves are cleared by the next save
- Only the lock holder writes the company file: doSave refuses when owner.json has another token (LOCK_LOST); a file whose saveId moved on is never overwritten — changes go to one 'unsaved changes' copy (companyFileSave.doSave / reusableConflictCopy)
- A working copy holding work the file lacks is never discarded: state.unsavedSince (noteUnsaved, also while the lock is lost), crashedWithUnsavedBooks (never-closed copy whose log moved on), pruneWorkingCopies skips unsaved copies and only removes data/ + state.json
- Only the working copy's own data folder is ever saved into the company file (SaveState.dataDir fixed at start; a sandbox switch is never saved there)
- Per-PC / per-location files never travel: .auth-secret, locks, clock probes, sandbox marker excluded from the company file (isExcludedPath); FILE_LOCAL_FILES excluded from backups and kept on restore; copies of a file carry no backup history
- A database or backup from a newer layout is refused before anything is changed: openDb refuseNewerFile, manifest dbSchema (prepareWorkingCopy NEWER_FORMAT), backup dbSchemaAt (validateBackupZip, readCompanyBackup, extractBackupArchive)
- Restore never goes live unverified: extract aside, swap, verifyRestoredBooks, roll back on failure; a pre-op safety copy first; the open single file's autosave pauses meanwhile (restoreState)
- Rotation deletes only versions recorded in this file's history and visible on this PC (rotateVersions)
- Lock protocol: mkdir is the lock; takeover only of the lock judged stale (same token); access requests never create the lock folder (NOT_IN_USE)
- Hosting: app server bound to 127.0.0.1; proxy strips x-bka-internal and stamps x-bka-remote-addr; LAN mode only private peers, pinned self-signed cert
- Whole-file operations (restore, backup download, condense archive restore, sandbox, portable) require an Admin of every company (requireFileAdmin) and exclusive access; all area routes are in lib/permissions/routes.ts
- Next launch's config (other file, No Company Open, /signup) is written only after the open file closed (configAfterClose)

### Known limits

- AI provider keys are encrypted with the per-data-folder .auth-secret, which never travels: they are lost on every other PC and on the same PC whenever its working copy is re-extracted (handed off to security)
- Two PCs taking over the same stale lock in the same instant can both briefly hold it (rm-then-mkdir); the saveId check keeps the loser's work in a conflict copy, but the protocol is not atomic
- The same company file opened through two paths (mapped drive vs UNC) gets two working copies; unsaved work in one only surfaces (as a conflict copy) when opened through that path again
- Server-side restore/preview/download use adm-zip in memory: backups or company files over about 2 GB cannot be restored from the Back Up and Restore page (restore-as-new-file via the shell is positioned now)
- snapshotDir reads every database file into memory synchronously on each save (memory spike and server pause on very large files); attachments are read lazily
- Pre-op safety copies of a single company file stay on the PC that made them (inside its working-copy folder, never pruned now) and are not visible from other PCs
- Backup history travels with the file: 'Last backup' and the schedule clock count other PCs' backups this PC cannot see, so a PC may skip its own scheduled backup
- Unsaved-changes copies keep the original's .file-id (sessions/archives keyed by it are shared with the copy)
- v1.6.0 (already released) has no newer-layout guard; files are protected only from this version on
- Office LAN hosting treats 100.64.0.0/10 (also ISP CGNAT) as tailnet and listens on 0.0.0.0

## 12. Sign-in, permissions, the API surface and the network policy

Auth, sessions, permissions, the API surface, the network policy and AI routing form the gate every request passes before it reaches a service. A company FILE is one PGlite data directory (a single .ironbooks file opens into a working copy). The directory holds several companies plus the file-level tables users, user_companies (membership, legacy role, is_active), user_role_assignments and company_roles (Enterprise roles), user_sessions, single_user_mode, password_history, file_secrets (new in 0072) and ai_settings. Company-level secrets and policies live in companies.settings: fileLock {enabled, hash}, passwordPolicy and closingDatePasswordHash on the oldest ('primary') company, and networkPolicy per company. A request works like this. middleware.ts only checks that the cookies are present and stamps the server-observed path and method (x-bka-route-path/method). getServerContext (lib/context.ts) then: (1) checks the file password (bka_unlock cookie, bound to the data dir and the launch nonce); (2) verifies the HMAC session cookie (lib/auth.ts, per-PC .auth-secret), which is bound to the file (.file-id), to this launch (ln = hash of BKA_INTERNAL_TOKEN) and to a live user_sessions row (sid); (3) refuses a password that must be changed; (4) picks the selected active, non-archived membership; (5) refuses others while single-user mode is held; (6) resolves effective access (the union of roles, lib/services/access.ts); (7) enforces the fail-closed route map (lib/permissions/routes.ts via enforce.ts); (8) applies the feature-switch and Accountant's Copy gates; (9) accepts the x-closing-password override only for roles with the closedPeriod flag, now rate-limited. Routes that act on the USER rather than a company ('self': company list/select/rename/archive, 2FA, challenge, change-password) use getSessionGate (file lock, file-bound session, deactivation, password-change gate). Rename and archive also check single-user mode for the company in the URL. Public routes (login, signup, unlock, reset, company-file/info, portal login, receipt phone link, sandbox/email status) do their own checks. Sign-in, unlock, portal, reset, 2FA-disable, challenge, change-password and closing-date guesses are rate-limited (lib/net/rateLimit.ts), with local and remote counters kept apart. Teammates on other PCs reach the server only through the host proxy, which always sets x-bka-remote-addr and strips x-bka-internal. Remote requests never get the first-run fallback, reset tokens or self-signup. Services re-check fine-grained rules: escalation and last-Admin rules in users.ts, document areas in attachments.ts, register account areas, assistant tool areas, maskForContext for SSNs and account numbers, and stripSecrets for credential material on every answer. This area posts to the ledger only indirectly: approved AI proposals go through journal.createManualEntry, register rows go through their document services, AI auto-apply goes through categorize, and the closing-date override decides whether closed periods may change. Every outbound connection asks lib/net/guard.ts (policyFetch / assertOutboundAllowed, purpose ai/email/vendor-lookup/updates), which uses the syntactic host classes in lib/net/cidr.ts plus DNS-verified *.ts.net names. AI calls go through lib/ai/router.ts with per-task policies (local-only by default). Cloud providers need both the company's cloudEnabled switch and the Privacy & Network ai opt-in, and they always get messages redacted by buildCompanyRedactor (every name list). Provider API keys are sealed as 'v2:' blobs with the file's own vault secret (file_secrets) so they travel with the file. Legacy 'v1:' blobs (made with the per-PC .auth-secret) are re-encrypted on first open on the PC that made them. Session signing stays per-PC.

### Rules that always hold

- Every API path+method is in the route map or is denied (lib/permissions/routes.ts + enforce.ts; routes.test.ts walks all route files)
- Stamped route headers always come from middleware (overwritten); with no stamp only an Admin passes (enforce.ts)
- A session cookie is valid only for this file (fid), this launch (ln), its live user_sessions row (sid) and kind 'user'; portal tokens are bound to the current portal password (lib/auth.ts)
- The file password gates every API route and sign-in, including 'self' routes (getServerContext/getSessionGate/login/reset/signup)
- Deactivated or archived memberships never select a company; a password that must be changed blocks everything but change-password (lib/context.ts)
- Single-user mode refuses every other session in that company, including rename/archive of it (sessions.assertSessionAllowed from context.ts and companies/[id]/route.ts)
- No privilege escalation: non-Admin user managers grant or edit only subsets of their own access; only an Admin grants Admin; the last active Admin can't be removed (users.ts, inside a transaction)
- A shared login's name or password can be changed only by the person or by an Admin of every company they belong to (users.assertCanChangeSharedLogin)
- File-wide operations need an active Admin of every company in the file (access.requireFileAdmin)
- Password hashes, answer hashes, TOTP secrets, reset tokens, sealed API keys and the file-password hash never appear in an API answer (stripSecrets via maskForContext and the company routes); SSN, tax id and account numbers are masked unless flagged (maskSensitive)
- Every password the app sets follows the file policy (signup, reset, admin reset, change-own, employee portal) and every password change is audited without the password
- Every password-like secret is rate-limited: sign-in, unlock, portal, reset request and token, 2FA disable, challenge, change-password, closing-date override
- Remote (hosted) requests never get the first-run fallback, self-signup, reset tokens or the internal token (requestInfo + hosting proxy)
- Nothing leaves the machine unless guard.ts allows the purpose for the host class under the company's mode and the env ceiling; redirects are re-checked and cross-origin hops lose credentials and bodies
- A provider is 'local' (unredacted) only when its host is loopback/LAN/verified tailnet per lib/net/cidr.ts; cloud providers need cloudEnabled plus the ai opt-in and always get redacted text (router.ts)
- Stored AI keys are sealed with the file's vault secret, bound to the company id (AAD); session signing never uses the vault secret (keyVault.ts)
- Sign-up happens once: the 'no real user' check, the Admin, the company and the session commit together under an advisory lock (signup route)

### Known limits

- companies.settings is read-modify-written by many writers outside one transaction (updateCompany, fileLock.writeLock, setClosingDate, stampLastOpened, archiveCompany, password policy). A concurrent Preferences save can silently undo a network or password policy, file password or archive change (not reproduced in a deterministic test; handed off)
- updateCompany audits the whole settings object, so closing-date and file-password hashes are STORED in audit_logs (answers now strip them, but the rows still hold them)
- Accountant's Copy route gate does not allow /api/company-file/save (Save Now) or /api/single-user (handed off)
- A sole Admin who loses their authenticator has no recovery (no backup codes); other users can now be recovered by an Admin
- The local rate-limit address key 'local' is shared by everyone on the host PC: one person's failed sign-ins lock other local sign-ins for a minute
- Host classification is syntactic plus *.ts.net DNS verification: a 'public' name that resolves to a LAN address is still treated as cloud (safe direction); verified ts.net names are trusted for 60 seconds
- buildCompanyRedactor builds one regex per name, address, phone and account across all lists on every cloud call (a performance cliff on very large files); vendor printOnCheckAs, customer resale numbers and job names are not entity-redacted (pattern detectors only)
- Adding a user whose login already exists in another company of the file attaches it and ignores a typed password (an earlier round's deliberate design, tested in users.retest.test.ts); the UI does not say so
- TOTP codes can be reused inside their 90-second window (no last-used counter)
- The employee portal is exempt from the file password and the launch binding by design (30-day portal sessions)

## 13. The transaction forms, centers and registers (UI)

The UI area is ~37 client pages (app/<form>/page.tsx, all 'use client') plus shared components and a handful of pure client libs. - A late async answer never replaces a newer one (Pay Bills vendor load, receive-bill preview, price quotes, reconcile totals) — sequence refs
- Pickers offer only accounts the document may use: no A/R / A/P / UF / inventory in Deposit To or Transfer Funds, banks for checks, deposit targets for payments (lib/accountTypes.ts)
- Keyboard: every QB key is resolved by pure functions in lib/shortcuts.ts; open pickers (aria-expanded) own Enter / arrows; dialogs own Esc

### Known limits

- No DOM test environment: page wiring is covered by pure helpers and source assertions only; an e2e (scripts/e2e*.mjs) needs a packaged build
- Bank Review fetches match candidates with one request per unreviewed row and repeats it after every Add / Match / Exclude — slow for a few hundred statement rows
- Previous / Next on Receive Payments only walks the current page of 50 payments (and the page's filters)
- Modal Esc / backdrop / × close a form without asking about unsaved changes (only Previous / Next / Revert ask)
- Edit forms reached through Previous / Next can open void / paid documents the list does not offer to edit; the save is then refused by the server
- Servers still accept the accounts the UI now hides (deposits to A/R / inventory, transfers touching A/R / A/P) — see handoffs
- usePriceQuotes / TransactionNav baseline rely on timing (900 ms snapshot); late async form fills can trigger a needless 'Discard changes?' or 'form changed' message

## 14. The app shell, navigation, keyboard system and reports UI

Team uib owns the application shell and every page not owned by uia. app/layout.tsx mounts WindowTitleBar (the frameless desktop title bar that shows the Electron application menu as native popups and opens them on Alt+letter), ThemeSync, DesktopBridge (Electron IPC: menu actions, 'navigate' routes, update banner), SandboxBanner, AppShell, Toaster and ClosingPasswordProvider. AppShell renders the sidebar from lib/nav.ts. There are two menu styles: Simple, and All menus (QB groupings). Links are filtered by the user's permissions (pageAreasFor) and by preference feature switches (featureHiddenPaths). The page search uses searchPages plus the plain-language intents in lib/nav-actions.ts. The header has the 'What is this page?' help (lib/pageHelp.ts via components/Help.tsx), the command bar button, Ask AI, Appearance and the company chip. AppShell also mounts the always-on keyboard layer. GlobalShortcuts handles QB navigation keys (Ctrl+W/I/J/A/R/T/F), the '?' help, 'Leave this form?' (mightLoseWork) for those keys, for NAVIGATE_REQUEST_EVENT from the desktop menus and command bar (navigateGuarded), and for F5. FormShortcuts handles Ctrl+N/Ctrl+S, Alt+S/A/R/P/N, Ctrl+Enter and Esc by finding buttons by data-shortcut or label (pickShortcutButton). KeyboardCommands handles Ctrl+E/D/P/Y/H/Q/L and F1 against the highlighted list row or the open form; when nothing has focus it forwards list keys to the page's first list. CommandPalette is Ctrl+K. UndoToast handles Ctrl+Z, and PreferenceBehaviors handles 'Enter moves between fields'. All pure key logic lives in lib/shortcuts.ts: QuickMath in exact decimals, QB date keys, doc-number +/-, globalShortcutFor, formShortcutFor, gridKeyAction/useGridKeys, and listKeyAction/useListKeys with type-ahead. useListKeys is wired into every <Table> in components/ui.tsx, so every list, center and report table gets QB list keys. Rows opt into commands with data-quick-report, data-journal-entry, data-row-open and data-list-label. Native Electron menus (electron/main.js + electron/menuRoutes.js) only display QB accelerators (registerAccelerator:false); the page's handlers own the keys, and the reload role is moved to F5 so Ctrl+R stays QB's register key. The pages themselves (dashboard, reports UI, settings/preferences, users & roles, security, privacy, backup, company file, multi-user, companies, accountant tools, payroll pages, lists, budgets, jobs, leads, letters, mileage, time tracking, onboarding, sandbox, qb-import, portal, phone receipt upload) are thin clients. They call API routes guarded by lib/permissions/routes.ts and never post to the ledger directly; all accounting happens in lib/services. Their correctness risks are wrong defaults (dates, periods, numbers), stale client caches, error and empty states, keyboard reachability, and destructive actions without confirmation. The reports UI runs every catalog report through StdReportView. Settings live in the URL so they can be shared and the Back button works. ReportFilters loads name lists from /api/reports/filter-options with a 5 s shared cache. QuickZoom and explanations go through StdReportTable and AmountExplain. Memorized reports link through app/reports/_components/memorizedHref.ts, and the Report Builder loads ?mem=. Exports use lib/csv (formula-safe) or /api/export/reports.

### Rules that always hold

- QB global keys work from inside fields except those marked inFields:false (Ctrl+A select-all, Ctrl+E/Ctrl+Q row commands) — globalShortcutFor(e, editable).
- No native Electron accelerator may take a QuickBooks key: menu shortcuts are displayed with registerAccelerator:false, Redo is Ctrl+Shift+Z, reload is F5, Minimize has no shortcut (electron/main.js, menuRoutes.js).
- Leaving a page with typed work (a data-modal dialog with fields, or a focused field in main) always asks first — for the QB keys, the desktop menus, the command bar and F5 (mightLoseWork + navigateGuarded/NAVIGATE_REQUEST_EVENT + isReloadKey in AppShell GlobalShortcuts).
- Amount math typed in fields is evaluated in exact decimals and rounded like Money.round2 (evaluateAmountDecimal/formatAmountResult); no eval.
- Form keys press only a clear target: explicit data-shortcut wins, else label rank; on a bare page Save requires a unique match; Ctrl+D in a form never presses a line's Remove (pickShortcutButton/pickCommandButton).
- A row's Delete/Remove/Void reachable by Ctrl+D must open a confirmation (QB always asks) — the pages own that confirmation.
- Line grids take Ctrl+D/Ctrl+Insert/Enter/arrows before the global commands (React handler + defaultPrevented checks).
- ?next= targets are resolved like the browser does and must stay on the app origin (safeAppPath).
- Report and Find name lists are reloaded at least every 5 s of use (FILTER_OPTIONS_TTL_MS), never once per session.
- Date defaults (today, this year, filing periods) are computed in the local calendar when a form opens, never in UTC and never at module load (shellUi.review5 scanner over owned pages).
- Sidebar, page search, palette and Home diagram filtering is cosmetic; every API route is enforced server-side (lib/permissions/routes.ts).

### Known limits

- The F5 guard relies on Electron on Windows firing menu accelerators only for keys the page does not preventDefault; if a build fires the accelerator anyway, F5 still reloads (no worse than before).
- mightLoseWork is a heuristic: a page-level form (not a modal) loses its guard once focus moves to the title-bar menu with the mouse.
- isEditableTarget counts checkboxes/radios as text fields, so Ctrl+E/Ctrl+Q/'?'/Ctrl+Z do nothing while a checkbox has focus.
- Menu mnemonics (Vendors = Alt+N) and the ';' / "'" month direction follow long-standing third-party lists; Intuit's current article disagrees, so they could not be confirmed.
- Missing QB keys: Ctrl+Alt+Y/V copy/paste line, Ctrl+G go to the transfer's register, Ctrl+U use list item, F2 product info; Ctrl+R opens the register list, not the highlighted account's register.
- Pages are thin clients tested mostly through extracted pure helpers and source assertions (no DOM test environment); runtime keyboard flows are only covered by scripts/e2e-keyboard.mjs against a packaged build.
- Esc and backdrop clicks close a Modal (components/ui.tsx) without checking for unsaved changes.
